Why the outreach dips in December: small businesses go quiet from mid December, and anything started after about 11 Dec will not be paid before you leave. December effort shifts to follow-ups and closing.

### Content rhythm each week
- Mon: pick the 3 posts (1 case study, 1 useful tip for small businesses, 1 behind the scenes or before and after).
- Tue to Thu: make and post one a day. Cross-post to Facebook Page and LinkedIn Company Page the same day. One Google Business Profile update a week (it helps local search).
- Reply to every comment within the day.

### Lead rhythm each week
- Mon: add 25 to 30 names to `Growth/leads/`, checked by hand (Google profile, Instagram, website).
- Tue to Thu: send 8 to 10 personal messages a day. Instagram DM for businesses that live on Instagram, email where there is a public business email. Never phone.
- Log every message in `Growth/pipeline.csv` the same day.
